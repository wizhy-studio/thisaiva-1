ABOUT THISAIVA

Catalysing Substantial Change in Venture Capital

Thisaiva is a sophisticated digital platform designed to facilitate the seamless raising of funds from your network for investment in private enterprises. Offering a comprehensive suite of features, it serves as your all-in-one solution for managing investment projects, from initial structuring to ongoing investor reporting.

Join a community of astute investors, as prominent figures in the investment landscape utilize Thisaiva to raise and deploy funds efficiently. We firmly believe that this initiative will catalyse substantial change within the venture capital landscape, fostering a more equitable environment where a diverse spectrum of entrepreneurs can readily access vital capital resources.

  • Streamlined fundraising campaigns from global accredited networks
  • All-in-one SPV structuring and transparent investor reporting
  • Equitable and accessible capital deployment for modern entrepreneurs
