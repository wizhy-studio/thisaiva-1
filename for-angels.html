Seamless Co-Investment Facilitation

Leveraging Thisaiva's Wealth Containers, our platform streamlines SPV structuring, ensuring a smooth and simplified co-investment experience for you and your network.

Maximizing Value for Greater Rewards

Harness the potential of subsequent prospects with Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) to continually elevate your investment journey to new heights, capturing value at every stage of the venture growth cycle.

Expand Your Investment Portfolio

Collaborate to access a wider array of opportunities by either collectively investing in smaller ticket sizes across multiple businesses or participating in larger deals with higher minimums, diversifying risk intelligently.

Earn Compensation for Your Contributions

Syndicate empowers you to receive a Carried Interest (performance fee) on successful investments shared with your peers, ensuring equitable rewards for your deal flow, curation, and leadership.

Maintain Long-Term Investment Strategy

With Syndicate, retain your investments until their potential fruition, utilizing pre-emption rights to navigate through various funding rounds, thus mitigating dilution risks commonly encountered by angel investors. Companies will value your steadfast commitment and ongoing support throughout their growth trajectory.

Automated SPV Entity Lifecycle

From instant digital subscriptions and automated KYC/AML checks to ongoing bank account provisioning and annual K-1 tax filings, Thisaiva handles all administrative burdens so you can focus purely on deal sourcing.
